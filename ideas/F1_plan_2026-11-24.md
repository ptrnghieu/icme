## 2. Where F1 stands

- **Strong:** CONSTRUCT beats GREEDY, TOPK and MBR on 3 sets (P14′ HOLDS).
- **Positive but not significant:** CONSTRUCT-D vs the calibrated aggregation rules of the two challenge winners (Agent-MER, MultiMER): +1.0 to +1.4 on every set, CIs include 0 (P16).
- **Below SOTA:** OV-MERD+ 69.27 vs MER-R1 70.68 (RL, 16×H100). Our number also carries the three deviations above.

**Contribution, strongest form:**
- A training-free, metric-derived decoder for generative MER.
- Beats standard decoding by a wide margin, and the hand-tuned aggregation of both challenge winners, on 3+ datasets and 2 backbones.
- Brings a 3B zero-shot model within about 1 point of RL-trained SOTA.

**Weakest form that remains if step B fails:**
- Matches hand-tuned challenge heuristics without hand rules, and beats standard decoding.
- Judged too weak for C3 on its own.

**Protocol P17 (to be pre-registered before the run):**
- Phase 1, MLLM = Qwen2.5-Omni-7B thinker, bf16, zero-shot:
  - inputs: 8 frames ≤ 336 px, audio, English subtitle for the MER sets;
  - official OV-MER prompt;
  - greedy + 16 samples (temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95);
  - description cut at the first line.
- Phase 2, extractor = Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, vLLM bf16, exactly as `AffectGPT-RL/statistic.py`:
  - `reason_to_openset_qwen` prompt from AffectGPT-RL, with the "at most four labels" sentence; user message only;
  - temperature 0.7, top_p 0.8, repetition_penalty 1.05, max_tokens 512;
  - `func_postprocess_qwen`, then `string_to_list`; no translation.
- Data: OV-MERD+ 532, MER2025-FG 1,000, EmoS-MELD 2,732 (all clips with a non-empty GT).
- Scoring: `p17_score.py` (5-fold cross-fitting, pooled primary test).
- Sensitivity: re-extraction with the older AffectGPT prompt (no four-label limit).
